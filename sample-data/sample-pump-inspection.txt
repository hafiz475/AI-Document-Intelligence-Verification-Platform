Equipment: Cooling Water Pump P-204
Manufacturer: Northstar Process Systems
Model: CWP-40
Inspection date: 2026-11-20
Location: Cooling Water Plant, Bay 2

Operating measurement
Drive-end bearing vibration measured 8.4 mm/s RMS during the loaded test.
The normal operating range is below 4.5 mm/s RMS. The measurement is therefore an
abnormal vibration condition requiring maintenance review.

Inspection observations
The pump casing was intact and no external leakage was observed. Motor current remained
stable. A light intermittent noise was present near the drive-end bearing housing.

Recommended maintenance
Inspect bearing alignment and lubrication before continued service. Repeat the vibration
measurement after maintenance and record the result in the equipment history.

General plant notes
The north access corridor was repainted last month. The weekly safety meeting remains
scheduled for Tuesday. These notes are unrelated to the equipment condition.
